About me

I'm a Software Engineer currently doing my MS in Computer Science at USC. Before this, I spent over a year at Happiest Minds building full-stack apps and backend services that real people actually used — 10K+ of them, in fact — which taught me a lot about what "production-ready" really means versus what looks good in a demo.

🤖 These days I'm most interested in where backend engineering meets applied AI. I like the kind of problems where you have to sit with the mess for a while before the clean solution shows up — and I'd rather spend an extra day understanding why something's broken than duct-tape a fix that'll bite me later.

🎾 When I'm not at a keyboard, I'm probably on a tennis or squash court. Both scratch a similar itch to engineering, honestly — you're constantly reading the situation, adjusting on the fly, and trying not to panic when your first plan stops working.
